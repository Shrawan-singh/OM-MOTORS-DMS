### DEMONSTRATION A: TRACTOR FINANCING & RECEIVABLES LIFECYCLE
* **Timestamp / Duration:** 06:30 - 07:30
* **Visual Cue:** Split screen showing Sales Invoicing (Image 10) and Accounts Receivables (Image 11 & 12).
* **On-Screen Graphic / Callout:** Live Workflow: Finance & Receivables
* **Spoken Narration:**
  > "Let’s walk through a financing lifecycle. Step one: Sales creates an invoice for an eight lakh rupee tractor. Step two: The farmer pays a thirty thousand rupee cash down-payment; the balance updates to seven lakh seventy thousand. Step three: The bank sanctions a seven lakh loan. The Accountant records the RTGS payout, dropping the receivable balance to seventy thousand. Step four: The farmer promises twenty thousand on harvest date, so we set that Promise Date. Step five: On the due date, the farmer pays twenty thousand via UPI, bringing the balance to fifty thousand. Step six: The farmer pays the final fifty thousand in cash. The account transitions to 'PAID / SETTLED' with a zero balance."

---

### DEMONSTRATION B: INWARD → PDI → SALE → WARRANTY → SERVICE LIFECYCLE
* **Timestamp / Duration:** 07:30 - 08:45
* **Visual Cue:** Rapid sequential cuts of Inventory (Image 07), PDI (Image 15), Invoice (Image 10), and Service Job (Image 13).
* **On-Screen Graphic / Callout:** Live Workflow: Stock to Service
* **Spoken Narration:**
  > "Finally, watch the end-to-end asset lifecycle. Step one: A tractor is inwarded into Inventory with Chassis SN-9812 as 'PDI PENDING'. Step two: The inspector completes the checklist, marking it 'PASSED' and 'AVAILABLE FOR SALE'. Step three: A customer purchases the tractor, receiving a 2-Year OEM Warranty. Step four: Three months later, the tractor returns for service with a hydraulic leak at 120 hours. Step five: The Service Manager looks up Chassis SN-9812, confirming active warranty. Step six: The technician replaces a customer-billed oil filter and a defective hydraulic seal covered under warranty. Step seven: The system automatically decrements one filter and one seal from inventory. Step eight: An approved invoice is issued, showing zero charge for the warranty items. That is how we run a tight, profitable dealership."
